# Buildto Customer App

Customer Flutter mobile application for **Buildto** - on-demand construction materials ordering and direct site delivery platform (cement, steel & TMT bars, bricks & blocks, sand & aggregates, plumbing, electricals, paints and hardware).

- **App Name:** Buildto
- **Package Name:** `in.buildto.customer`
- **Design System:** `packages/kraveo_ui`
- **Branding:** `branding/brand.json`
